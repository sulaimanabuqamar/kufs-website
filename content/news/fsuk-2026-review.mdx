We finished 24th overall at Formula Student UK 2026, out of 96 classified teams. That is
31 places better than last year and the first time this team has finished inside the top
quarter of the field. It is worth saying plainly: everyone who touched this car earned
that result.

It is also worth being honest about the rest of it.

## Where the points came from

Design took 92 of 150. The judges singled out the suspension kinematics work and, to our
mild surprise, the cost report — two years of unglamorous documentation discipline
finally showing up on a scoresheet. Business Plan Presentation took 61 of 75.

On track, we completed endurance for the second year running. Not quickly, but we
completed it, and roughly half the field did not.

## The three things that cost us

**Scrutineering, day one.** We failed tilt on the first attempt over a fuel system vent
routing that we had changed three days before we left. Six hours of the schedule gone,
and it pushed our first dynamic run into a wet session. This was entirely self-inflicted
and is the easiest 20 places on this list to recover.

**Acceleration.** 4.62 s. The launch calibration had never been run on a surface with
Silverstone's grip level because we never tested on one. We are budgeting for two test
days on comparable tarmac before we travel in 2027.

**Driver hours.** Our top driver had 140 km of seat time before the event. Teams
finishing in the top ten routinely arrive with three times that. No amount of design
margin closes that gap.

## What we are changing

The 2027 programme moves shakedown forward by six weeks, which is the single biggest
lever we have on all three of the problems above. The milestone dates on our
[progress page](/progress) reflect that.
