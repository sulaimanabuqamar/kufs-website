The 2027 campaign starts properly this week. Concept freeze went through at the end
of May, the chassis design review cleared in July, and the first plies go down on the
monocoque in August.

## What changes this season

Three things came out of our post-season review, and all three are structural rather
than technical.

**Shakedown moves forward by six weeks.** Every problem we have had at competition has
traced back to not having enough running time before we travelled. Moving first
running into May, rather than late in the summer, is the single biggest lever we have.

**Business and cost get owned properly.** Three of the eight scored events at Formula
Student UK are static, and they are won on documentation and commercial reasoning
rather than on lap time. We are recruiting specifically into those roles this year
instead of treating them as something the engineers pick up.

**We publish the schedule.** The milestone dates on our [progress page](/progress) are
the real ones, and we will report against them — including the ones that slip. Our
partners are entitled to the actual picture, not a highlights reel.

## What we need

Recruitment opens at the start of the autumn term across every subteam. If you want to
be part of it, the [join page](/join) has the open roles.

If you build things we need — machining, composites, materials, software, logistics —
the [sponsorship page](/become-a-sponsor) explains how that works. A large share of
Formula Student support is parts rather than cash, and it is often worth more to us.
